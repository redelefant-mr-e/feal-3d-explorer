What is this?

This application is a simple UX design study for an interactive FEAL product explorer. It shows a two-part, vehicle-mounted loading ramp (IRV242) in a 3D environment. In this early study, the vehicle is represented by a simple rectangular block.

How does it work?

You can orbit the view and zoom in or out with drag, scroll, or pinch gestures. Four positions are predefined:

  • Stowed: ramp in transport position
  • Partially open: slightly open position
  • Presentation: intermediate display pose
  • Fully deployed: ramp fully unfolded

Each position is triggered by the buttons below the explorer. Animations between steps keep the transition smooth; your viewing angle stays the same when the pose changes.

Limitations

This is a very early prototype and should be treated as a study. The following have intentionally been left out and could be future milestones:

  • Materials and textures
  • FEAL logo
  • Background environment
  • Photorealistic lighting
  • Embedded product dimensions
  • Vehicle design
